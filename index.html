Focus

Built with structure and staying power.

I build full stack products and software systems where interface, data, and operations need to work together clearly. The approach is practical and durable: clean architecture, usable interfaces, and decisions that still make sense as the product grows.

The work spans commerce platforms, school systems, management tools, and workflow driven products for teams that need software to do real operational work. That includes products shaped around payments, records, dashboards, approvals, and client facing journeys.

I work across React, TypeScript, Node, Express, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Redis, and payment integrations, choosing the stack to fit the system rather than forcing one pattern onto every build.
